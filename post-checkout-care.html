How this was measured so you can trust it

  • Base = 77 reachable, not 200 entered. Excluded: 100 filtered at the first check (already returning / rebooked / member) and 23 that couldn't be messaged because their checkout event carried no service id — so no refill booking link could be built and every send was skipped.
  • "Rebooked after a nudge" = 26. Clients who received at least one message and then booked. Clients who'd already booked before any message were excluded from the win — the journey just detected the booking and exited them.
  • Scope. The 3 live production journeys — Greentree Washington Wexford. Staging/test copies excluded.
  • Delivery & clicks are from the send log and the tracked-link click table; opt-outs are real STOP replies.
  • Still assisted, not A/B. "Booked after a nudge" is strong attribution but not a controlled test — some of the 26 might have returned anyway.
  • Pulled live from production on 13 Oct 2026 (previous pull 10 Oct: 70 reachable, 20 rebooked). The campaign is still enrolling, and numbers rise as the 36 active clients finish their sequence. The no-link cases are under separate root-cause review for a system fix.
