How this was measured
  • Go-live: 28 Nov 2025 for Wexford and 30 Nov 2025 for Greentree, the day Zoca connected each profile and started posting.
  • Click windows: the 30 days before go-live, against 9 Sep to 8 Oct 2026, the last 30 full days of Google data. Same 30 days last year is 9 Sep to 8 Oct 2025.
  • Website clicks and profile clicks come from each location's Google Business Profile performance data. Profile clicks are views of the profile from Google Maps and Search, desktop and mobile.
  • Not showing means outside the results everywhere on the rank grid.
